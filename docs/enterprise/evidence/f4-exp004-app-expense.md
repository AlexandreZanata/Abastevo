## Observed before any change

- The Kotlin `ExpenseOperations.submit`/`read` and the shared oracle existed
  (EXP-002) but no producer, store, owner or screen: the device could not
  queue an `expense.submit`.
- The Go intake keeps envelope attachments and `trip_id` RETRY/UNAVAILABLE;
  the DOC-003 registry had no `EXPENSE` owner (the OFFL-005 attachment
  session refusal test and the DOC-003 tests named `EXPENSE` as the
  unregistered kind) and `org_document_links` had no expense column.
- `DocumentTargetKind.EXPENSE` already exists in Kotlin and Go (it is the
  allocatable kind); the OFFL-005 owner gate accepts any queued operation of
  the same partition as owner.
