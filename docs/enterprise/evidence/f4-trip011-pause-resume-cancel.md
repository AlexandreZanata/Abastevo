## Rules frozen before behaviour

Rules B-BR-EMP-390..393 and BUC-EMP-197..198 are in the
[product contract](../../product/ABASTEVO_EMPRESAS_CONTRACT.md) section
"EMP-TRIP-011 — Pause, resume and cancellation (2026-11-15)"; decisions
EDR-252 (pure lifecycle commands, operational revision, failure order, finish
from `PAUSED`, `trip-v2.psv`) and EDR-253 (three signed operations, outcome
mapping, migration 000233, reserved logbook types unchanged, submission of a
cancelled trip refused) are in [16-decisions](../16-decisions.md). They are
the recommended routine decisions, taken unattended:

- **Own operations, not logbook types.** A logbook fact never changes its trip
  (TRIP-003) and the shared device vectors pin `trip.event.record` PAUSE /
  RESUME / CANCEL as unsupported, so the lifecycle has three signed operations
  on the trip entity; the reserved types keep their RETRY/UNAVAILABLE answer.
- **Revision.** A trip is revision 1 after its start and one more per lifecycle
  command or finish; each command names the revision it expects (state-model
  rule 7). A finish is acknowledged at the trip revision after it (2 without
  lifecycle commands, unchanged for existing trips).
- **Outcomes.** A closed trip holds the command `TRIP_ALREADY_CLOSED`; a stale
  revision, or a state that no longer admits the command, holds it
  `STALE_REVISION`; shape, a missing cancellation reason and an instant before
  the start are `MALFORMED`; another member is `FORBIDDEN`; a trip not stored
  here is `RETRY`/`DEPENDENCY_PENDING`.
- **Assets.** A paused trip holds its asset (one-active-trip index, open work,
  active use, TRIP-013 running check, `ASSET_BUSY` re-proof); a cancelled trip
  releases it at its cancellation, has no finish, keeps every earlier fact and
  reports usage `NOT_MEASURED`/`CANCELLED`.
- **Cancellation authority.** C1 stays driver-initiated: only the member who
  started the trip pauses, resumes or cancels it. A supervisor cancellation
  (`trip.trip.close_for_other`) belongs to EMP-TRIP-014.
