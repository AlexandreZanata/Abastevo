## Scope and blocker

ESP-11 is a hard dependency for official-record insights and remains blocked:
FQ-06/07 live runtime, safe station association and the selected official-record
acceptance are OWED. This phase therefore implements only the independent
price/metrics insights and downloads. It makes **no** enforcement/fine claim and
does not mark ESP-11 accepted.

## Delivered

- `/curiosidades/` with a fixed approved question catalog (no generative
  article, forecast or “worst station” label):
  1. how many stations had a price and how source rows were included;
  2. national gasoline minimum/maximum comparison between two revisions, or an
     explicit sample-change explanation when the station set changed;
  3. fuel/unit station coverage;
  4. municipalities with at least five priced stations versus insufficient
     samples;
  5. source/collection period and Abastevo publication;
  6. exclusion census with each discarded-row reason.
  Every card shows its formula and a methodology/source link. The page is
  print-friendly through the existing `@media print` rules.
- `/dados/` with bounded exports from the latest revision only:
  `estacoes.csv`, `precos.csv`, `resumos.csv` and a JSON download manifest with
  byte/row counts, SHA-256, attribution, period and notes.
- CSV fields are allowlisted and public; no CPF, user photo, contributor GPS,
  internal note or raw document. UTF-8 output; cells beginning with `=`, `+`,
  `-`, `@` or tab are prefixed with an apostrophe; CR/LF are removed. The
  manifest and page state the pending redistribution/precision gates.
- `/curiosidades/` and `/dados/` are generated by `portalgen` from the frozen
  snapshot/analytics; downloads and pages are included in the portal manifest.
  The shared navigation/footer expose both routes. Candidate builds remain
  `noindex`.
- No new dependency, API, database call, source activation, official-record
  record or public release was added.

## Limitations

- Official-record insights and any fine/enforcement claim remain blocked by
  ESP-11; the page does not substitute synthetic official records.
- Downloads cover the latest revision only; bounded history, national full-dump
  absence and CSV byte budgets are intentional. Real-scale download sizes were
  not measured.
- The real ANP edition remains `BLOCKED_OVER_PRECISION` (ESP-07) and rights are
  `RIGHTS_PENDING`, so these pages/downloads are synthetic fixtures in candidate
  builds and are not published.
- Print support is CSS-only; no separate print route or PDF service exists.
- No real browser/low-memory/HTTPS validation was performed in this phase.
- Next: ESP-13 artifact verification is the next independent phase; ESP-11
  remains the gating phase for official-record publication.
