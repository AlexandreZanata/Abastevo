the monthly report must show bought energy, charge cost, compatible
money/km and month/category/vehicle breakdowns with honest unknown

suppress invalid ratios; bounded projection rebuild.

## Delivered

- `backend/internal/modules/metrics/energy.go`: pure `EnergyInterval`
  (bought Wh, paid totals, shared distance, gap/segment/odo/SOC/mixed
  flags) with exact sum-first ratios (`WhPerKm`, `CentavosPerKm`) and
  `CombinedCostPerKm` (fuel purchase plus stated charge totals, counted
  once each; fees excluded, inclusion unknown). No new dependency.
- `backend/internal/modules/ledger/application/ports.go`: `ReportTotals`
  gains `charge_energy_wh/cost_centavos/fees_centavos`,
  `energy_coverage` and `combined_cost_per_km` (ratio or unknown reason
  per vehicle). `ByVehicle` keeps all vehicle-attributed money (fuel
  plus charge plus expense, one row counted once).
- `backend/internal/modules/ledger/adapters/reports.go`: `BuildReport`
  aggregates the `charge` dataset (stated values only, nulls stay out),
  applies the PHEV shared-distance basis, bumps the projection to
  version 3 (old cached totals invalidate), and refuses unknown datasets
  instead of misclassifying them as expenses (previous `else` branch).
- `backend/internal/modules/ledger/adapters/http/reports.go`: CSV gains
  charge total lines plus per-vehicle `energy:`/`combined:` lines
  (sorted, formula-escaped inputs only; no new route, no OpenAPI change:
  `LedgerReport.totals` is free-form).
- `DeclareGap` accepts `charge` anchors (EE-15 left refuel-only
  anchoring, leaving charge gaps inexpressible); `ReportRows`/`MetricRows`
  gap evidence joins both datasets (`IN ('refuel','charge')`, sqlc
  regenerated, Go signatures unchanged). Unknown gap IDs stay refused.
- `consent.go`: erasure revokes the `charge` bit too (mask 3→7; EE-15
  left charge writable after erase) and `SetConsent` reports `charge`
  in enabled datasets (previously silent).

## Limits

- Mixed (fuel plus charge) months refuse both per-carrier ratios
  conservatively, even when only one carrier moved the vehicle; the
  combined money/km and all money totals stay exact.
- Session fees are exposed separately, never added to session totals
  (their inclusion in the stated total is unknown by contract).
- Single-session windows report `distance-nonpositive` for efficiency
  (no invented distance); gap evidence anchored on charge records flags
  conservatively through the shared month window.
- Gap/segment reasons reuse the fuel vocabulary; SOC anomaly is new
  (`soc-inconsistent`) and applies to charge windows only.
- No device, no production, no deploy and no release acceptance is
  claimed.
