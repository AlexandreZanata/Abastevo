Before behavior: a signed-in free account can publish a dated
place/connector/price/condition/experience report, list community
reports, edit or withdraw its own report, vote on helpfulness and
report another account's observation. Reading stays anonymous and
free. No GPS, home context, route, photo or source key exists in the
draft or wire. A pending write survives offline/process restart under
the same client ID; a lost response retries verbatim, a stale edit
stays visibly conflicted, and another account's request/receipt is
never rendered.
