## Limits

- This unit prepares charging locations only. No vehicle references
  (EE-05), Go bridge (EE-06), tariffs, storage, publication, worker
  or app. The snapshot, preparer output and PII stay outside Git.
- Admission covers the three verified open licenses with attribution
  retained; any other provider, contact, comment, photo or private
  coordinate is excluded or quarantined, never adapted.
- Coordinates are exact source lexemes (not float conversions);
  powers are integer watts from finite positive kilowatts; quantities
  are reported group counts, never EVSE identities.
- A new snapshot revision needs re-approval with hashes (same
  pattern as MEX-17/20a); this acceptance pins commit `8e3bedca…`.
- No device, no production, no deploy and no release acceptance is
  claimed.
