- Kotlin Vehicle requires TankCapacity and FuelProduct, not propulsion/carriers.
  TankCapacity is positive BigDecimal up to200L, rounded to2 decimals. Room's
  vehicle entity persists tankCapacityLiters as Double and a non-null fuel string.
  Those legacy storage semantics must survive; don't copy them for exact energy
  or tariff arithmetic, force a BEV tank, or invent a battery on upgrade.

  litres/M3/KG_13 do not become Wh or kWh by reinterpretation.

  includes non-deleted retained registrations. No EV wire payload is implemented.
  MEX-01's staged vehicles.multiple/one-free amendment does not grant capabilities.
- Room16 has16 entities, including vehicle, owner-scoped Premium queues/cursors,
