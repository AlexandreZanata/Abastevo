## Frozen semantics implemented

- Eligibility is an input contract: a tariff and scenario must declare the
  same known condition and qualifier. STANDARD has no qualifier; APP/MEMBER/
  EMPLOYER require one; UNKNOWN or mismatched eligibility is refused as
  `ineligible-condition`, never silently priced as standard.
- Only `tax_basis=INCLUDED`, `mandatory_components_complete=true`, no
  undeclared minimum/maximum stage and `FINAL_HALF_UP_CENTAVO` produce a
  total. EXCLUDED/UNKNOWN tax, incomplete components, any non-null
  minimum/maximum (the frozen schema declares no stage/basis) and
  `SOURCE_SPECIFIC_UNSUPPORTED` return `UNKNOWN_FEES`.
- An absent ENERGY_WH component is `UNKNOWN_FEES`; an explicit `0/1`
  energy rate is a known zero. Paid parking alone never makes the whole
  visit free.
- Each component quantity is rounded UP to its `billing_step` only after
  its time grace (grace on non-time components is refused). SESSION
  requires quantity/step 1. Duplicate dimensions, non-canonical/out-of-bound
  rates, invalid step/grace, min>max and malformed identities are refused.
- Missing scenario quantities are refused (`missing-scenario-quantity`);
  they are never treated as zero. Bounds: energy 0..1e9 Wh, time
  0..31,622,400 s, money 0..1e12 centavos.
- The tariff validity is a half-open interval; a scenario window outside
  it, or crossing its end, is `MISMATCHED_WINDOW`. RFC3339 offsets compare
  as instants; IANA timezone allocation stays with EE-15/reporting.
- A single final half-up rounding applies to the exact rational sum; no
  per-component rounding. A result above 1e12 centavos is `OVERFLOW`.
- Wire `Calculation` shape for TARIFF_SCENARIO is exercised through the
  KNOWN/UNKNOWN outcomes; the engine does not emit other method/unit pairs.
