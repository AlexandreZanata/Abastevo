## Rollback and interrupted rollout

Stop new candidate admission through existing operational controls if the worker/API pair is inconsistent; keep private cleanup and safety processing running. Restore a known compatible API/worker pair that can still consume v2 receipts. Never roll back to a binary/schema that rejects outstanding v2 receipts: either retain v2-consumption support or wait for their immutable maximum deadlines and verify the drain. A replay returns the original receipt and never extends its camera/submission expiry.

Keep append-only schema history and authoritative observations/moderation/audit records. Rebuild projections through existing bounded processing; do not enqueue an unbounded bulk backfill or restore deleted evidence. Android rollback retains Room13 readability or requires an explicitly tested compatible build; do not downgrade the database destructively or delete outboxes/drafts. A stored helpfulness OFF preference remains respected. Old or unknown fresh capture policy produces a service recovery message, without requesting GPS or enabling developer preview.
