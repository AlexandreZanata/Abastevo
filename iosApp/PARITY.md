# Android and iOS functional parity

Baseline: Android and backend at `e77339125a71f07c3fbcdaed6a087a53ceff04f4`.

Acceptance is open. Each supported Android journey needs an equivalent iOS entry point, the same API or local business rule, and evidence for success, refusal, recovery and persistence where applicable. Use the same versioned fixtures in [contracts/testdata](../contracts/testdata). A screen, model, manifest declaration or sample result alone cannot satisfy acceptance.

## B-BR-IOS-ID / BUC-IOS-ID — Device identity and signed community writes

- Each signed request uses the backend profile: SHA-512 over the covered base, raw r||s P-256 signature as unpadded base64url, the canonical authority of the selected origin, and one fresh SIGN challenge. The content lines cover the exact body bytes. XCTest replays the Go identity vectors.
- A REGISTER challenge registers the device contributor once per process. With a live account session, the contributor is bound with `anpfuel-bind.v1` signed over SHA-256. The Keychain stores the contributor and key ids without iCloud synchronization. Android does not rotate keys, so iOS has no rotation flow.
- Sign in with Apple requests no scopes. Apple receives the SHA-256 hex of a random raw nonce. The backend receives the raw nonce, the identity token and the optional authorization code. Any login then registers and binds the device key.
- Price observations are device-signed and may be anonymous. Station comments and votes need the account session. Forum topics and comments need the session plus the bound device proof. One client id per write serves as the idempotency key or forum operation id.
- An evidence photo needs a `photo-capture-community-v2` grant for the same station and a camera capture inside its two-minute window. Gallery photos only feed local OCR. A lapsed grant or a photo older than 24 hours expires without upload. Pix has no backend condition and is refused locally. Device location is never sent.
- Outbox items are draft (waiting for sign-in), pending, accepted (with the receipt and server state), refused (with the server code) or expired. Offline, 401, 408, 425, 429 and 5xx failures retry with backoff and `Retry-After`, up to 25 attempts. Items from another owner or origin are refused. Logout and deletion purge the outbox and transient photos.

Android references are `PhotoProofTransport`, `AnonymousProofHttpClient`, `ContributionUploadHttpClient`, `PhotoCaptureHttpClient`, `FeedbackHttpClient` and `ForumHttpClient`. Acceptance needs staging runs for registration, binding, each write route, a refused write, an offline retry, evidence upload with expiry, owner history, and Sign in with Apple on a provisioned device.

## B-BR-IOS-ANP / BUC-IOS-ANP — Official survey weeks

- The week list comes from the public ANP listing page. A week appears only when its block links both the weekly summary and the station detail spreadsheet. Weeks are ordered newest first.
- A download accepts only HTTPS `www.gov.br` URLs under the ANP price path, before the request and again after redirects. The listing is capped at 4 MiB and each spreadsheet at 64 MiB while it is read. A spreadsheet must have an `.xlsx` name, an accepted content type and a ZIP signature. Each ZIP entry must match its declared size and CRC-32, and inflation is capped while it runs.
- The summary must belong to the chosen week. Rows with an unknown product or state label are quarantined and counted, never guessed. Prices round half up to centavos, like Android `PriceAmount`, and are kept as integer milli-BRL. Station rows are kept only for the selected city's state.
- Each stored week keeps the SHA-256 and size of both source files and both row files in Application Support, excluded from backup. A row file that no longer matches its manifest drops the week.
- The active week is the chosen downloaded week, otherwise the newest download. Data is stale eight days after the survey end. A manual sync that finds no newer week records a verification. A station-detail failure keeps the summary as a partial week.
- On foreground, a check runs at most once every seven days without expensive or constrained networks. When the newest week is not current for the selected state, it is downloaded and made active.
- City history needs at least two downloaded weeks for the fuel. A price-drop alert compares the cheapest station of the city, otherwise the city minimum, with the previous downloaded week. It notifies once per fuel and week, and asks for notification permission only when the user turns an alert on.

Android references: `AnpListingScraper`, `AnpFileDownloader`, `StreamingXlsxParser`, `PriceTableBatchImporter`, `WeekPickerViewModel`, `HomeViewModel.refresh`, `SyncWorker`, `HistoryViewModel` and `EvaluatePriceDropAlertsUseCase`. Acceptance downloads two real weeks, restarts in airplane mode and compares the averages with the ANP files.

## B-BR-IOS-EV / BUC-IOS-EV — Admitted charging catalog and trip planning

- A refresh reads the national profile coverage and then every page of the same revision. The place and group counts must match the coverage, and the edition must be unchanged at the end. The OCM profile is used only when the national read fails. A failed refresh keeps the saved catalog. Private fields, foreign group owners and malformed rows refuse the page.
- Only admitted coordinates create map pins or route candidates. A place without coordinates stays in the list. Availability, opening hours and tariffs are unknown, never live or zero. A snapshot from another API origin is hidden, and a snapshot older than 24 hours is labelled stale.
- Filters follow Android `ChargingCompatibility.matches`. In the national catalog, a minimum power filter excludes groups with unknown power.
- The planner ports Android `EvEnergyModel`, `EvChargingPlanRules` and `PlanEvTripUseCase`. Road legs come from MapKit, and a failed leg is a missing route, never a straight line. Candidates are national places within the 25 km route corridor that offer the declared vehicle connector, with one connector per site. Private places are never stops. Each stop charges to the target, and a failed first stop is replanned from its arrival charge without energy from that site.

Android references: `ChargingDiscoveryRepository`, `ChargingExploreViewModel`, `ChargingMarkersSource`, `RoomEvCandidateSource` and `EvTripViewModel`. XCTest replays `contracts/testdata/ev/trip-energy-v1.csv`.

## B-BR-IOS-EMP / BUC-IOS-EMP — Abastevo Empresas driver operations

- Company access uses the account cookie plane as Android `CompanyAccessClient` does. A live personal session opens `POST /v1/org-access/session/login`, which must return the single `__Host-empresas-session` cookie (Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, no Domain) and a CSRF token. The channel exists only in memory, is bound to the account and session family, and sends `Origin`, the cookie and `X-CSRF`. It lists and reads contexts, previews, accepts and declines invitations, and reads the applicable checklist. Context reads deny every 403/404 generically. An invitation needs the reviewed notice text, a code whose locator matches the receipt, and one operation id per decision, which a retry reuses.
- Entering a company needs an online server read of the membership, then device custody: signed `devices/check`, `devices/enroll` with label `iOS` when absent, and `devices/contact`. ACTIVE selects the company. LOCK keeps unsent work for delivery and accepts no new command. ERASE erases that partition alone. Generic refusals, outages and malformed answers never lock or erase. The selection is memory-only. Leaving or switching returns to the personal context. Records stay in the partition (account, origin, organization) that created them.
- Every company command and device read uses `SignedRequestTransport` with the account proof inside the signed body: `/sync/reference` (assigned assets), `/sync/operations` (`trip.start`, `trip.finish`, `expense.submit`, `check.run.complete`) and `/sync/attachments`. Receipt bytes then go to the capability-bound `/documents/{id}/content/upload` ingress. Operations are sealed in the enterprise canonical JSON profile with their payload digest. iOS sends `run_clock` null and records no trip track or location, so tracking stays off.
- The protected queue is a per-partition JSON file in Application Support, excluded from backup and file-protected on iOS. Commands are delivered in creation order, in batches of up to 10, and a finish waits for its start. Acknowledgements are bound to operation id and digest. RETRY and transport failures back off from 30 seconds, never shorter than `Retry-After`, capped at 6 hours. Offline failures do not count, and after 25 counted attempts a command pauses until the person retries. A receipt is sent only after its expense is received, and is refused locally if the expense was rejected. Only final receipts can be dismissed. Logout, account deletion and an origin switch purge all partitions. Opening company mode with another account erases what earlier accounts left.
- Refusals are shown as returned: sign-in, fresh authentication, invalid code, already a member, managed account, rate limit, entitlement, suspended membership or organization, revoked device and access denied. A refusal has no local effect on another organization or the personal context.

Not on iOS: missions, fuelings and fueling authorization notices, trip pause/resume/cancel and events, the return checklist and checklist photos, review-result polling, managed unlock and the encrypted vault generations of Android. Android keeps sealed work after logout; iOS purges it. The XCTest suite replays the canonical, operation, ACK, reference-feed and attachment-session vectors. Acceptance needs a staging run for invitation, selection, enrollment, assets, each command, a refused command, offline retry, receipt upload, LOCK/ERASE and logout purge on a physical iPhone.

## B-BR-IOS-02 / BUC-IOS-02 — Origin and training consent

- Switching origin requires a signed-out client with no retained account key. A session or recovery key issued for one origin must never be forwarded to a different origin.
- The training screen can show the current terms. Grant, status and revocation require the authenticated owner and observable server receipt under the existing privacy contract. The iOS release keeps data collection off and shows account consent management as unavailable until the signed transport and durable revocation are verified.

## B-BR-IOS-01 / BUC-IOS-01 — Driving routes and offline maps

- A driving route uses the selected station's routable coordinates and a current, user-requested location. Directions and distance come from the navigation provider. Missing coordinates, denied location, and a failed provider request produce an unavailable state. An external navigator can still open a routable destination.
- A regional package becomes available offline after download, manifest and checksum verification, and successful opening by the map engine. A saved identifier, display sample, or cached online tile cannot establish package availability. Abastevo's own regional packages need the Android map engine, so iOS shows them as unavailable.
- On iOS, offline turn-by-turn navigation uses the offline maps the user downloads in Apple Maps (iOS 17+). The Offline navigation sheet explains the download and opens the selected city in Apple Maps; the route sheet hands a routable station to Apple Maps for driving directions. The app does not report whether Apple Maps holds a region.

Android references: `NativeRouteViewModel`, the native map engine, and the map region download flow. Acceptance uses a valid station and a routable current location, then repeats with permission denied, a provider error, and no station pin. For offline acceptance, verify download, process restart in airplane mode, route creation, and deletion. The iOS route preview is one-shot. Foreground guidance, rerouting, and regional offline navigation require separate execution evidence.

## B-BR-IOS-VEHICLE / BUC-IOS-VEHICLE — Local vehicle and ledger records

- An unsigned local registration can add one vehicle. Additional vehicle admissions require the authority defined by Android's `VehicleAdmissionRule`. Previously saved vehicles remain readable and removable.
- A battery capacity is required for electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles. The decimal kWh input must resolve to a whole number of Wh between 1 and 1,000,000,000, inclusive. Comma and period are accepted decimal separators. Other propulsion types store no battery capacity.
- The local ledger requires the user to choose a registered vehicle. Refueling requires a supported fuel and charging requires compatible propulsion. Numeric fields use exact integer units on iOS. Invalid or out-of-bounds values prevent saving and keep the draft available for correction.
- An energy or cost-per-distance indicator requires qualified measurement intervals. The current iOS ledger record has no measurement completeness or segment boundary information, so the UI reports the indicators as unavailable.

Android references are `VehicleAdmissionRule`, `BatteryCapacity`, `LedgerDraft`, `PremiumBounds`, and `PremiumEnergy`. The corresponding shared fixtures are `contracts/testdata/premium/vehicle-registration-v2.json` and `contracts/testdata/premium/energy-v1.json`.

## B-BR-IOS-DRIVER / BUC-IOS-DRIVER — Manual local shifts

A new local shift requires the user's selection of a registered vehicle, a valid initial odometer reading, and no other active shift. Closing the selected shift records the actual completion timestamp and validates the final odometer, exact centavo amounts within the Android `DriverMoney` bound, and the manually declared duration. Malformed inputs leave the shift open for correction. This iOS flow stores manual local records. GPS, work accounting, and synchronization require their own integration and acceptance tests.

## B-BR-IOS-PREMIUM / BUC-STORE-01..04 — App Store Premium

- The [store billing contract](../docs/product/payments/store-billing.md) applies. The Premium sheet reads `POST /v1/premium/status`, shows the App Store products listed in its `app_store` offer with StoreKit's localized price and period, and purchases with the offer's `account_binding` as `appAccountToken`.
- A verified transaction is sent to `/v1/premium/purchases/verify` (or `/restore`) with `provider: app_store` and its `originalTransactionId`, signed by the device key bound to the account. The app finishes the transaction only after the server accepts or definitively refuses it; unavailable, quota, retry and session failures keep it unfinished for the `Transaction.updates` and `Transaction.unfinished` retries.
- Premium active from any source (web, Google Play, admin) hides the purchase button. Restore calls `AppStore.sync()` only from the explicit button. The sheet offers subscription management, renewal disclosures and the Terms and Privacy links.
- Acceptance still needs an App Store Connect subscription group and products, a sandbox tester run (purchase, Ask to Buy pending, renewal, refund, restore on a second device, another account refused) and a review build against the production server with the sandbox fallback on.
