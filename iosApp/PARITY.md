# Android and iOS functional parity

Baseline: Android and backend at `e77339125a71f07c3fbcdaed6a087a53ceff04f4`.

Acceptance is open. Each supported Android journey needs an equivalent iOS entry point, the same API or local business rule, and evidence for success, refusal, recovery and persistence where applicable. Use the same versioned fixtures in [contracts/testdata](../contracts/testdata). A screen, model, manifest declaration or sample result alone cannot satisfy acceptance.

## B-BR-IOS-I18N / BUC-IOS-I18N — Language and regional formats

- The app follows the operating system's preferred app language. English is the development language. The available languages match Android: English, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, German, French, Japanese, Russian and Simplified Chinese.
- App copy and permission prompts use String Catalogs. Shared labels use the matching Android resource translations where their meaning is the same. Display dates, numbers and units follow the regional format, and prices stay in BRL.
- Localization leaves signed request bytes, wire formats and the authoritative wording of hash-bound legal terms intact.

## B-BR-IOS-COMMUNITY / BUC-IOS-COMMUNITY — Community and forum

- Public forum and station-feedback lists use opaque cursors and preserve server order. A refresh replaces the loaded page and cursor together. Forum text search covers loaded posts. The app labels this scope. Threads show the server's parent and depth, with ranked top-level forum comments and on-demand replies. A newer filter, account, origin, refresh or navigation entry wins over an earlier response.
- Observation actions require the server's contribution reference and decision sequence. Confirmation and dispute use device proof and the same submission id on retry. Helpfulness accepts HELPFUL and NOT_HELPFUL, can be removed, and uses its own tally. The public tally omits the owner's choice. Ratings are separate, use integer stars from one to five, and keep the mean absent when there are no ratings.
- Station feedback writes use the existing session envelope. Comment edits carry the last server revision and normalized text of at most 280 Unicode scalars. Feedback replies have one level. The app exposes edit and removal for owner receipts it can verify. A creation with an unknown outcome needs an explicit reload before another attempt.
- Forum edit, removal, reaction and owner reads use the account session and bound device proof. Edits and removals use the owner revision. Forum reactions are one LIKE, DISLIKE or NONE per target with authoritative counts and a private owner read. Each command keeps its operation id when retried.
- Reports follow their specific forum, station-comment or user contract. The app names users through a public content reference. Server-side blocks are private to their owner. The app filters topics, comments and reply previews through the signed hidden-content subset, allows up to five seconds for the check, and rechecks after a block changes. Logout, deletion and origin changes clear the private UI state.

Registry provenance, dated ANP prices and community reports keep their own source labels. A missing source, count, price or pin stays absent. Cached street data and dated offline ANP data remain readable within their verified scope.
- PMQC shows the source revision and its published coverage and counts. Official enforcement uses the separate occurrence and establishment projections. Private quality reports use the account session and bound device proof. The report journal is scoped to account, origin and session family and holds the confirmed command for its bounded retry window.
- Fuel and charging suggestions use the existing session envelope. A fuel suggestion needs a name and accepts an optional checksum-validated CNPJ. A charger suggestion needs a name or a public address and carries no coordinates. Reuse the submission id for the same queued proposal. The private owner list, status and pending cancellation use the result returned by the server.
- Public commercial profile representation requires an admitted operator source and the verified badge from the backend. The owner interface uses the existing session routes and shows the integration-pending receipt when the server keeps the private routes closed. Proof uploads depend on the existing PDF validation and storage boundary.

## B-BR-IOS-ID / BUC-IOS-ID — Device identity and signed community writes

- Each signed request uses the backend profile: SHA-512 over the covered base, raw r||s P-256 signature as unpadded base64url, the canonical authority of the selected origin, and one fresh SIGN challenge. The content lines cover the exact body bytes. XCTest replays the Go identity vectors.
- A REGISTER challenge registers the device contributor once per process. With a live account session, the contributor is bound with `anpfuel-bind.v1` signed over SHA-256. The Keychain stores the contributor and key ids without iCloud synchronization. Android does not rotate keys, so iOS has no rotation flow.
- Sign in with Apple requests no scopes. Apple receives the SHA-256 hex of a random raw nonce. The backend receives the raw nonce, the identity token and the optional authorization code. Any login then registers and binds the device key.
- Price observations are device-signed and may be anonymous. Station comments and votes need the account session. Forum topics and comments need the session plus the bound device proof. One client id per write serves as the idempotency key or forum operation id.
- An evidence photo needs a `photo-capture-community-v2` grant for the same station and a camera capture inside its two-minute window. Gallery photos only feed local OCR. A lapsed grant or a photo older than 24 hours expires without upload. Pix has no backend condition and is refused locally. Device location is never sent.
- Outbox items are draft (waiting for sign-in), pending, accepted (with the receipt and server state), refused (with the server code) or expired. Offline, 401, 408, 425, 429 and 5xx failures retry with backoff and `Retry-After`, up to 25 attempts. Items from another owner or origin are refused. Logout and deletion purge the outbox and transient photos.

Android references are `PhotoProofTransport`, `AnonymousProofHttpClient`, `ContributionUploadHttpClient`, `PhotoCaptureHttpClient`, `FeedbackHttpClient` and `ForumHttpClient`. Acceptance needs staging runs for registration, binding, each write route, a refused write, an offline retry, evidence upload with expiry, owner history, and Sign in with Apple on a provisioned device.

## B-BR-IOS-ANP / BUC-IOS-ANP — Official survey weeks

- The week list comes from the public ANP listing page. A week appears only when its block links both the weekly summary and the station detail spreadsheet. Weeks are ordered newest first.
- A download accepts only HTTPS `www.gov.br` URLs under the ANP price path, before the request and again after redirects. The listing is capped at 4 MiB and each spreadsheet at 64 MiB while it is read. A spreadsheet must have an `.xlsx` name, an accepted content type and a ZIP signature. Each ZIP entry must match its declared size and CRC-32, and inflation is capped while it runs.
- The summary must belong to the chosen week. Rows with an unknown product or state label are quarantined and counted, never guessed. Prices round half up to centavos, like Android `PriceAmount`, and are kept as integer milli-BRL. Station rows are kept only for the selected city's state.
- Each stored week keeps the SHA-256 and size of both source files and both row files in Application Support, excluded from backup. A row file that no longer matches its manifest drops the week.
- The active week is the chosen downloaded week, otherwise the newest download. Data is stale eight days after the survey end. A manual sync that finds no newer week records a verification. A station-detail failure keeps the summary as a partial week.
- On foreground, a check runs at most once every seven days without expensive or constrained networks. When the newest week is not current for the selected state, it is downloaded and made active.
- City history needs at least two downloaded weeks for the fuel. A price-drop alert compares the cheapest station of the city, otherwise the city minimum, with the previous downloaded week. It notifies once per fuel and week, and asks for notification permission only when the user turns an alert on.

Android references: `AnpListingScraper`, `AnpFileDownloader`, `StreamingXlsxParser`, `PriceTableBatchImporter`, `WeekPickerViewModel`, `HomeViewModel.refresh`, `SyncWorker`, `HistoryViewModel` and `EvaluatePriceDropAlertsUseCase`. Acceptance downloads two real weeks, restarts in airplane mode and compares the averages with the ANP files.

## B-BR-IOS-EV / BUC-IOS-EV — Admitted charging catalog and trip planning

- A refresh reads the national profile coverage and then every page of the same revision. The place and group counts must match the coverage, and the edition must be unchanged at the end. The OCM profile is used only when the national read fails. A failed refresh keeps the saved catalog. Private fields, foreign group owners and malformed rows refuse the page.
- Only admitted coordinates create map pins or route candidates. A place without coordinates stays in the list. Availability, opening hours and tariffs are unknown, never live or zero. A snapshot from another API origin is hidden, and a snapshot older than 24 hours is labelled stale.
- Filters follow Android `ChargingCompatibility.matches`. In the national catalog, a minimum power filter excludes groups with unknown power.
- The planner ports Android `EvEnergyModel`, `EvChargingPlanRules` and `PlanEvTripUseCase`. Road legs come from MapKit, and a failed leg is a missing route, never a straight line. Candidates are national places within the 25 km route corridor that offer the declared vehicle connector, with one connector per site. Private places are never stops. Each stop charges to the target, and a failed first stop is replanned from its arrival charge without energy from that site.

Android references: `ChargingDiscoveryRepository`, `ChargingExploreViewModel`, `ChargingMarkersSource`, `RoomEvCandidateSource` and `EvTripViewModel`. XCTest replays `contracts/testdata/ev/trip-energy-v1.csv`.

## B-BR-IOS-EMP / BUC-IOS-EMP — Abastevo Empresas driver operations

- Company access uses the account cookie plane as Android `CompanyAccessClient` does. A live personal session opens `POST /v1/org-access/session/login`, which must return the single `__Host-empresas-session` cookie (Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, no Domain) and a CSRF token. The channel exists only in memory, is bound to the account and session family, and sends `Origin`, the cookie and `X-CSRF`. It lists and reads contexts, previews, accepts and declines invitations, and reads the applicable checklist. Context reads deny every 403/404 generically. An invitation needs the reviewed notice text, a code whose locator matches the receipt, and one operation id per decision, which a retry reuses.
- Entering a company needs an online server read of the membership, then device custody: signed `devices/check`, `devices/enroll` with label `iOS` when absent, and `devices/contact`. ACTIVE selects the company. LOCK keeps unsent work for delivery and accepts no new command. ERASE erases that partition alone. Generic refusals, outages and malformed answers never lock or erase. The selection is memory-only. Leaving or switching returns to the personal context. Records stay in the partition (account, origin, organization) that created them.
- Every company command and device read uses `SignedRequestTransport` with the account proof inside the signed body: `/sync/reference` (assigned assets), `/sync/operations` (`trip.start`, `trip.finish`, `expense.submit`, `check.run.complete`) and `/sync/attachments`. Receipt bytes then go to the capability-bound `/documents/{id}/content/upload` ingress. Operations are sealed in the enterprise canonical JSON profile with their payload digest. iOS sends `run_clock` null and records no trip track or location, so tracking stays off.
- The protected queue is a per-partition JSON file in Application Support, excluded from backup and file-protected on iOS. Commands are delivered in creation order, in batches of up to 10, and a finish waits for its start. Acknowledgements are bound to operation id and digest. RETRY and transport failures back off from 30 seconds, never shorter than `Retry-After`, capped at 6 hours. Offline failures do not count, and after 25 counted attempts a command pauses until the person retries. A receipt is sent only after its expense is received, and is refused locally if the expense was rejected. Only final receipts can be dismissed. Logout, account deletion and an origin switch purge all partitions. Opening company mode with another account erases what earlier accounts left.
- Refusals are shown as returned: sign-in, fresh authentication, invalid code, already a member, managed account, rate limit, entitlement, suspended membership or organization, revoked device and access denied. A refusal has no local effect on another organization or the personal context.

Not on iOS: missions, fuelings and fueling authorization notices, trip pause/resume/cancel and events, the return checklist and checklist photos, review-result polling, managed unlock and the encrypted vault generations of Android. Android keeps sealed work after logout; iOS purges it. The XCTest suite replays the canonical, operation, ACK, reference-feed and attachment-session vectors. Acceptance needs a staging run for invitation, selection, enrollment, assets, each command, a refused command, offline retry, receipt upload, LOCK/ERASE and logout purge on a physical iPhone.

## B-BR-IOS-02 / BUC-IOS-02 — Account export, origin and training consent

- Switching origin requires a signed-out client with no retained account key. A session or recovery key issued for one origin must never be forwarded to a different origin.
- The server export is a single private JSON document built from current owner reads. Each section records its data, availability and coverage limitations. Walk all supported cursors until completion and keep previously admitted pages if a later page fails. Report capped, device-specific, lookup-only and unavailable sections explicitly. Do not export credentials or use a record identifier to change owners. Bind the export to account, session family and origin until the user shares it. The separate local JSON export stays available.
- Android has no server account export. iOS reads cloud datasets and devices, Premium history and private ledger records, contributor observations, accessible forum receipts, station claim and suggestion history, the current training preference and blocked accounts. Some owner services expose only exact identifiers, fixed limits or the current device contributor. These limitations must appear in the export manifest.
- Training preference uses the same device proof as Android, under a live account and exact origin. The reviewed Portuguese terms are hash-bound. A grant requires reading and confirming the current terms and a matching revision receipt. Revocation saves a protected, scope-bound intent before sending an idempotent command. Local pending status does not claim deletion, and retry reuses the same id and revision unless the server has definitively refused the revision. Logout, account deletion and origin switch invalidate receipts, clear the pending journal and fence in-flight reads.
- The terms endpoint reports that training collection is disabled. The Android per-photo training row has no production caller, and the photo proof transport signs ordinary evidence uploads separately from training preference. iOS keeps training collection and sample uploads off.

Android references: `TrainingConsentHttpClient`, `TrainingConsentViewModel`, `TrainingConsentHostViewModel`, `TrainingConsentRevocationWorker` and `PhotoProofTransport`. Verify grant, stale terms, offline revocation, retry, account and origin switching, and each export section against staging before claiming acceptance.

## B-BR-IOS-01 / BUC-IOS-01 — Map search, saved places and driving guidance

- Search scopes Abastevo stations to the selected municipality and uses the backend station query. An admitted charging site keeps its own source and pin policy. Apple place search runs separately and cannot establish an Abastevo station identity. Recenter only for one eligible match or an explicit eligible selection.
- Saved places and GPX exchange are private to the current owner and origin. Admit a complete UTF-8 GPX file only after checking format, depth, size and point quotas. Refuse DTDs, external entities and unknown formats. An import is atomic and idempotent. A deleted ID requires explicit consent before re-import. A guest cannot read an account's saved places.
- Preview uses a routable destination and a fresh requested fix or explicit origin. MapKit provides the car route and maneuvers. Continuous updates start only after the user starts guidance and remain in memory. Apply the Android accuracy, freshness and 60-second reroute rules. MapKit's provider-independent progress leaves the route after three distinct good fixes beyond the greater of 30 m and twice reported accuracy. Arrival requires two good fixes within 20 m of the destination and route end, at accuracy of 25 m or better.
- Request When-In-Use permission and show the location indicator. Continue only the active session while the app is in the background, and duck audio during each spoken instruction with the app language voice. Stop on arrival, explicit end, permission loss, logout, deletion or origin switch. Guidance fixes never go to the Abastevo API. Apple Maps remains an explicit handoff.
- A regional package becomes available offline after download, manifest and checksum verification, and successful opening by the map engine. A saved identifier, display sample, or cached online tile cannot establish package availability. Abastevo's own regional packages need the Android map engine, so iOS shows them as unavailable.
- On iOS, offline turn-by-turn navigation uses the offline maps the user downloads in Apple Maps (iOS 17+). The Offline navigation sheet explains the download and opens the selected city in Apple Maps; the route sheet hands a routable station to Apple Maps for driving directions. The app does not report whether Apple Maps holds a region.

Android references: `MapDiscoveryViewModel`, `SavedViewModel`, `GeoExchange`, `StrictXml`, `NativeRouteViewModel`, `GuidanceViewModel`, `GuidanceService`, `GuidancePolicy` and `LocalGuidanceVoice`. Acceptance needs station and Apple search, safe GPX import/export, a good driving fix, a deliberate deviation, GPS loss, permission denial, lock-screen voice and background updates, provider error and explicit end. Verify offline download and routing in Apple Maps on the physical device.

## B-BR-IOS-VEHICLE / BUC-IOS-VEHICLE — Vehicles, ledger records and ledger sync

- One new local vehicle is free, including guests. A second or third follows Android's `VehicleAdmissionRule`: `vehicles.multiple` from a server Premium status seen within 900 seconds, or a lease bounded by min(seen + 24 h, paid-through) for the same account and origin. The ceiling stays three. Held vehicles stay readable and removable, and removal keeps their records.
- A battery capacity is required for electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles. The decimal kWh input must resolve to a whole number of Wh between 1 and 1,000,000,000, inclusive. Comma and period are accepted decimal separators. Other propulsion types store no battery capacity.
- A record matches the backend refuel, expense or charge payload in exact units: centavos, milli-litres or milli-cubic-metres, Wh and metres. It has a stable lowercase UUID, the event instant and IANA zone, and a full, partial or unknown fill. The user selects a registered vehicle. Refuels need fuel propulsion; the fuel defaults to the vehicle's (LPG has none). Charges need electric propulsion. Inputs never round, and blank optional fields stay absent. Two local markers stay on the device because no route accepts them: an odometer reset or replacement, and a declared missed fill.
- Records from the first iOS ledger decode forward with nothing lost. The day is kept at local noon, the fill is unknown, an old expense becomes `other`, and a zero charge energy means not measured. Odometer-only readings stay local. Refuels take the vehicle fuel. Each migrated record needs owner review before upload, and its odometer counts as unverified (`odometer-unit-unverified`) until saved.
- Monthly indicators port `GetMonthlyIndicatorsUseCase`: half-open months in the device zone, full-to-full consumption and purchase cost per km, combined fuel-plus-charge cost over one shared distance, measured and reference Wh/km, and the change against the previous month. Undecidable results keep the frozen UNKNOWN reason. iOS also applies its segment marker (`odometer-segment-boundary`) and applies gaps and segments to the baseline month.
- Sync is per account and origin. The queue, acknowledged revisions, conflicts, tombstones, cursors and the lease are purged at logout and deletion. Opt-in sets server consent through `/v1/ledger/consent`. Commands go one at a time to `/v1/ledger/commands`, signed with the bound device key, with deterministic operation IDs (Java `nameUUIDFromBytes`) and the acknowledged revision. A 409 parks the record with base, current (read through `/v1/ledger/records`) and proposed versions. The owner keeps their version at the current revision, takes the server version, or decides later. A 403 pauses paid uploads, keeps local data and lets deletes through. 400 and 404 refuse without retry. Other failures retry with backoff and `Retry-After`.
- Deletes are free tombstones. A pull pages `/v1/ledger/records` with the opaque cursor, saving it per page and restarting on an expired one. It adds missing records and adopts newer revisions of records without local changes. It never resurrects a delete or overwrites pending work. CSV export and `/v1/ledger/erase` are free; after erasure, records stay on the device and are not uploaded again.

Android references are `VehicleAdmissionRule`, `BatteryCapacity`, `LedgerDraft`, `PremiumBounds`, `PremiumConsumption`, `PremiumEnergy`, `GetMonthlyIndicatorsUseCase`, `DispatchLedgerCommandUseCase` and `ResolveLedgerConflictUseCase`. The XCTest fixtures copy `contracts/testdata/premium/{financial-execution-v1,energy-execution-v1,financial-wire-v1}.csv` and `vehicle-registration-v2.json` verbatim, and the integrity test checks them line by line. Acceptance needs a staging run for consent, upload, refusal, 409 resolution, a lapse, pull, erase and two devices, plus a device run of the screens.

## B-BR-IOS-VEHICLE-DETAIL / BUC-IOS-VEHICLE-DETAIL — Vehicle edits and local analysis

- Editing keeps the registered vehicle identifier and associated records and revalidates its propulsion, fuel and battery. The device-only photo is decoded and re-encoded as a bounded JPEG with no source metadata. The protected photo, estimate and alert preferences belong to the exact account or guest and origin. Vehicle removal clears its preferences and photo; account deletion, logout and origin switch purge their protected scope.
- Per-vehicle price-drop alerts use the vehicle's admitted fuel and the same verified current and previous ANP week. Only an enabled rule requests notification permission. Deliver at most once per eligible vehicle and week. Estimate input uses exact decimal parser limits and remains distinguishable from measured efficiency. The monthly CSV keeps exact values and missing-data reasons; the raw record export remains available.

## B-BR-IOS-ANALYSIS / BUC-IOS-ANALYSIS — Server reports, comparisons and alerts

- Android has no client for these routes. Build and CSV call the bound-device ledger report routes after the server admits the account's Premium capability. Read the retained report with its live owner session even after a lapse. Bind report and CSV receipts to the submitted month and timezone. CSV uses the server bytes and ShareLink without a stored artifact.
- Compare selected vehicle periods using the signed metrics route and its implemented fuel-purchase method. Keep exact server rational values, render incomplete and incompatible reasons, and omit a change or ratio the server did not establish.
- Advanced alert reads, inbox, deletions and erasure remain available when Premium lapses. Enabled paid writes require server capability. Each command keeps its operation ID and eligibility through bounded retries. Notifications request explicit local permission and use a generic inbox message for trusted dispatched rows. Server evaluation, dispatch scheduling and APNs delivery require their runtime integration. The app shows the delivery boundary and keeps free reads and deletes available.

## B-BR-IOS-DRIVER / BUC-IOS-DRIVER — Manual local shifts

A new local shift requires the user's selection of a registered vehicle, a valid initial odometer reading, and no other active shift. Closing the selected shift records the actual completion timestamp and validates the final odometer, exact centavo amounts within the Android `DriverMoney` bound, and the manually declared duration. Malformed inputs leave the shift open for correction. This iOS flow stores manual local records. GPS, work accounting, and synchronization require their own integration and acceptance tests.

## B-BR-IOS-PREMIUM / BUC-STORE-01..04 — App Store Premium

- The [store billing contract](../docs/product/payments/store-billing.md) applies. The Premium sheet reads `POST /v1/premium/status`, shows the App Store products listed in its `app_store` offer with StoreKit's localized price and period, and purchases with the offer's `account_binding` as `appAccountToken`.
- A verified transaction is sent to `/v1/premium/purchases/verify` (or `/restore`) with `provider: app_store` and its `originalTransactionId`, signed by the device key bound to the account. The app finishes the transaction only after the server accepts or definitively refuses it; unavailable, quota, retry and session failures keep it unfinished for the `Transaction.updates` and `Transaction.unfinished` retries.
- Premium active from any source (web, Google Play, admin) hides the purchase button. Restore calls `AppStore.sync()` only from the explicit button. The sheet offers subscription management, renewal disclosures and the Terms and Privacy links.
- Acceptance still needs an App Store Connect subscription group and products, a sandbox tester run (purchase, Ask to Buy pending, renewal, refund, restore on a second device, another account refused) and a review build against the production server with the sandbox fallback on.
