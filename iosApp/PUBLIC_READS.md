# Public reads on iOS

- **B-BR-IOS-READ-01.** Display directory records from the selected Abastevo origin and IBGE municipality. A record without coordinates in an accepted pin tier remains in the directory and stays ineligible for map pins or routing.
- **B-BR-IOS-READ-02.** Display dated official prices only from the station's ANP weekly observations. City averages, survey downloads and historical charts require admitted data for that scope. Missing sources have an explicit unavailable state.
- **B-BR-IOS-READ-03.** Decode the community feed using its canonical fuel, condition, currency and publication time. Keep its projections separate from official prices. Switching the origin, city or fuel cancels the previous read and prevents an older response from reopening its scope.
- **BUC-IOS-READ-01.** A user can browse the directory on the selected origin, request the next page, and open a station's dated official prices. A valid empty response and an unavailable API produce different states.
- **B-BR-IOS-READ-04.** The last successful directory page, community feed and station official prices are cached per API origin and city with their save time. When the API is unreachable or offline mode is on, the app shows the cached rows with their age. A community price is kept only until its server `expires_at`. Expired rows are counted but never shown. When no cached station price exists, the station dossier uses the downloaded ANP week matched by CNPJ and names that week.
- **BR-016 / BR-017.** Onboarding and the city picker share Android's bundled [IBGE municipality asset](../data/src/main/assets/ibge_municipalities.json) and retain selections by IBGE code. Name matching ignores accents and supports filtering by UF. When a city cannot be read from the catalog, require a manual city selection before requesting data. One-shot GPS centers the map without inferring a city from unrelated sample centroids. City coverage does not imply that ANP or directory records exist for that city.

- **B-BR-IOS-READ-05.** The station dossier binds directory identity, registry sources and official prices to the same canonical station. Verified station identity enables contribution and representation actions. Registry status, the survey price and community observations keep their source and date. The map uses only accepted pin tiers.
- **B-BR-IOS-READ-06.** The forum and community lists follow server cursors. Forum search filters the loaded posts. Dated observations use the canonical contribution and decision projection. The signed personal block lookup applies the owner's hidden-content subset to the public rows.
- **B-BR-IOS-READ-07.** PMQC reads use the station, fuel and source revision for assays, samples, coverage and provenance. The official-record browser admits enforcement records through the source-supported document and station filters. Company associations carry their own label. An unavailable or stale source has an explicit state, and coverage counters retain the server's definitions.

The public quality cache respects the source's cache policy, caps retention at seven days, reuses its ETag and displays saved responses as stale when their freshness cannot be confirmed. Private reports, owner lists and moderation state require their own live account proof. Station and Apple place search, survey downloads, city history, EV catalog and offline navigation are described in the [iOS parity contract](PARITY.md).
