## How the math works

The fuel cost of a trip depends on three numbers: the distance, the vehicle's
consumption and the price per liter.

```
liters = distance ÷ consumption (km/l)
fuel cost = liters × price per liter
total cost = fuel cost + tolls
```

With **Round trip** checked, the distance is doubled. Tolls are counted as you
enter them: add up every toll plaza on the route you will drive (outbound,
return or both). To split the bill, the total is divided by the number of
people.

## Worked example

A trip of 430 km one way, driven as a round trip, in a car that does 12 km/l,
with fuel at R$ 6.29 per liter, R$ 45.80 in tolls in total and two people
splitting the cost:

1. Total distance: 430 × 2 = 860 km.
2. Liters: 860 ÷ 12 = 71.7 liters.
3. Fuel: 71.67 × 6.29 = R$ 450.78.
4. Total with tolls: 450.78 + 45.80 = R$ 496.58.
5. Per person: 496.58 ÷ 2 = R$ 248.29.

The values are illustrative and only serve to show the math.

## The Brazilian context

Prices are per liter in Brazilian reais (BRL), and the price on the sign
already includes federal and state taxes. At most stations an attendant fills
the tank for you; you do not operate the pump. Pix, Brazil's instant payment
system, and cards are common payment methods.

## Limits of the estimate

- Consumption on the highway is usually different from consumption in the
  city. Use the consumption you measured on stretches similar to the trip.
- Load, air conditioning, speed, terrain and traffic change the result. It is
  worth adding a margin when you plan the budget.
- The fuel price changes along the way. Check the
  [reference prices by state](/precos/) (in Portuguese) to get an idea of the
  regions you will pass through, always looking at the collection date.

## In the app

The abastevo Android app, still in development, helps with the price and with
the stretch to the station:

1. With a vehicle registered in "Vehicles", the "Tank fill cost" card on the
   home screen shows the week's reference price applied to your tank.
2. On a station's details screen, "Route and go" draws the route to the
   station and shows the distance and the time. For the whole trip, use the
   distance of your route in the calculator on this page.
