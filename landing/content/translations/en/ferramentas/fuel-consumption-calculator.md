## How the math works

The most reliable way to measure consumption is the tank-to-tank method: fill
the tank, reset the trip odometer, drive normally and fill up again. The
liters added in the second fill-up are what the car used over that distance.

```
consumption (km/l) = kilometers driven ÷ liters added
liters per 100 km = liters ÷ kilometers × 100
cost per km = price per liter ÷ consumption (km/l)
```

The full step-by-step, with the precautions that keep the measurement
accurate, is in the guide
[how to calculate your car's average consumption](/guias/como-calcular-consumo-medio/)
(in Portuguese).

## Worked example

After a fill-up, the trip odometer read 412 km. Filling the tank again took
35.6 liters, at R$ 6.29 per liter:

1. Consumption: 412 ÷ 35.6 = 11.6 km/l.
2. Liters per 100 km: 35.6 ÷ 412 × 100 = 8.6 liters.
3. Cost per km: 6.29 ÷ 11.57 = R$ 0.544.
4. Spent at the fill-up: 35.6 × 6.29 = R$ 223.92.

The values are illustrative.

## The Brazilian context

Prices are per liter in Brazilian reais (BRL), and the price on the sign
already includes federal and state taxes. At most stations an attendant fills
the tank for you; you do not operate the pump.

## Limits of the estimate

- A single measurement can mislead. Repeat it over three or four tanks and use
  the average.
- The point at which the pump nozzle shuts off varies; refuel at the same pump
  whenever possible, and do not top off beyond the first automatic shut-off.
- The consumption shown on the car's dashboard is an estimate from the trip
  computer and can differ from the math done with the actual liters.

## In the app

The abastevo Android app, still in development, stores each vehicle's
fill-ups so you have the numbers for this math at hand:

1. In "Vehicles", tap the list icon next to the vehicle and choose "Refuel".
2. In "New entry", enter the "Quantity (L)", check "Full" and note the
   "Odometer (km)".
3. Tap "Save". With two full-tank fill-ups in "Entries", use the kilometers
   driven and the liters in the calculator on this page.

The entries stay on the device only. The captures are on the Portuguese page,
show the Portuguese interface and were taken on November 17, 2026. Their
example fill-up uses fictitious values: 40 L, a full tank, R$ 250.00 in total
and an odometer reading of 12,500 km. See the tutorial
[Vehicles and tank estimate](/como-usar/veiculos/) (in Portuguese).
