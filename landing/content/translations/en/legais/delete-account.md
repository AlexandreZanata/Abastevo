## Delete your account in the app

1. Open **Profile**, then **Manage account**.
2. Select **Delete my account**.
3. Read the confirmation and tap **Yes, delete**.

You can use this option without contacting support. You need access to your own account to confirm it. If you are offline or have no server confirmation, the deletion is not confirmed.

## What to provide and how we protect your request

Say the request concerns Abastevo, describe your request and give only the reference needed to locate the account. Support may guide you through a proportionate identity check to prevent someone else from deleting your data.

**Do not send passwords, access keys, private keys or authentication codes.** Do not post your request, username, documents or evidence in GitHub issues or comments. If you lost a credential, explain that to support. Knowing a username alone is not proof of account ownership.

Exercising your rights with the controller is free. You will receive instructions about verification, progress, expected completion and any information that must be kept on legitimate grounds.

## What happens to your data

- **Account and access.** When the server receives a valid request, credentials are revoked and account deletion and identity unlinking begin. Acknowledging a request does not certify that every physical copy has been cleared.
- **Associated personal data.** Where applicable, we delete or anonymize personal data, including links to contributions and personal data in the content. This is more than a temporary account lock.
- **Evidence photos.** These are temporary evidence, with an operational access window of up to 24 hours from first receipt. The 24-hour rule does not apply to every app file.
- **Public history.** Effectively anonymous prices and statistics may remain. Public-source and legally retained information are treated separately.
- **Security, documents and legal duties.** Records strictly necessary to prevent fraud, defend rights or comply with law may be retained. We explain their category, purpose and applicable period or criterion.
- **Backups and other devices.** Deletion of restricted copies follows the retention and recovery cycle without putting deleted data back into use. Files you exported and powered-off devices depend on local controls or the destination where the copy resides.

See [retention periods and criteria](/en/privacy/#8-how-long-we-keep-data) and [other privacy rights](/en/privacy/#9-how-to-exercise-your-rights). You can ask which procedures have finished and which are still in progress.

## Delete specific data or revoke a choice

You can ask to correct or delete specific data without deleting your whole account. For optional choices, use the app's privacy controls or contact support. A revocation sent offline may remain pending until it is received.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your server account. Account deletion does not automatically cancel a subscription. Use the controls provided by the store or provider you used to purchase it and ask for guidance if needed. You still have the right to request deletion of unnecessary personal data.
