## 1. Purpose of the project

Abastevo is a community platform with publicly available source code for sharing and consulting fuel price information in Brazil. We do not sell or broker fuel and are unaffiliated with regulatory authorities such as the ANP.

## 2. Community guidelines and contributions

When you submit fuel prices, photos, ratings, comments, replies or forum topics through the app:

- You declare that the information reflects genuine observations.
- Comments and other free text are subject to the platform's length limits and rules for civil conduct.

You must not post or submit any of the following in a community area:

- Spam, chain messages, unauthorized advertising or repeated content intended to attract attention.
- Insults, threats, harassment, intimidation or stalking.
- Hate speech or discrimination based on race, color, ethnicity, origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or any other status.
- Sexual or pornographic content, graphic violence or encouragement of violence or self-harm.
- Illegal content or content encouraging crimes, scams, fraud or sales of prohibited products and services.
- Defamation, slander or false allegations against people or businesses.
- Other people's personal data, such as addresses, phone numbers, documents or vehicle license plates.
- Content that infringes copyrights, trademarks or other third-party rights.
- False price information or attempts to manipulate confidence in prices, votes or ratings.
- Accounts that impersonate another person, company, fuel station or public authority.

## 3. Moderation, reporting and blocking

Abastevo provides tools to report irregularities, abuse and inaccurate data. In the app, with an account, you can:

- **Report content** using its own options menu. This covers forum topics, comments and replies, station comments and charging observations.
- **Report a user** from the options menu of any content they published, providing your reason.
- **Block a user.** Their topics, comments and replies will disappear from your forum and station pages. The blocked person is not notified. You can unblock them at any time in **Profile → Manage account → Blocked users**.

Forum topics and comments are reviewed before publication. People on the moderation team review reports. They may hide or remove content and restrict participation by people who breach these terms. Moderation follows human and transparent procedures. Community reports and downvotes do not automatically delete content without prior review. The reported person is not told who submitted the report.
