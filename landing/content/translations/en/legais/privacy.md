**Version 1.0 · Updated November 5, 2026 · Android app:** `com.abastevo` (Play Store) / `com.anpfuel.app` (GitHub).

This policy explains what data the site and app use, what stays on your device, when information is sent to the service, and how to exercise your rights.

## Before you continue

- You can look up public prices without an account and select your city manually.
- The account, camera and location are used for actions that require them. Refusing a permission does not authorize other uses of your data.
- Your financial records and private trips are not posts for the community.
- Ask for access, correction or deletion through a private channel. You do not have to publish your data on GitHub.

## 2. What data we use and why

Processing depends on what you do and which features your version offers. Technical identifiers and usernames may still be personal data even when they do not show your legal name.

- **Website and app searches.** City, state, fuel, filters, map area and queries needed to find stations and prices. Public searches require no registration. The server and network providers may receive IP address, date and time, requested URL, browser or app type and technical details needed to connect and protect the service. A term in a URL may also be recorded in browser history or access logs.
- **Accounts and authentication.** Username, account or installation identifiers, protected credentials, sessions and security records are used to create, access and protect accounts. Where email or provider login is offered, we also use the email address and identifiers required for sign-in. We do not receive your Google or Apple account password. A pseudonymous identity does not make all those data anonymous.
- **Community participation.** We use the station, fuel, price, terms, date, corrections, comments, confirmations, ratings and reports you submit, plus data needed to establish authorship, prevent abuse and review disputes. Community content and aggregate counts may be public. Individual votes, reporters' identities, credentials and contributors' precise coordinates are not presented as public data.
- **Vehicles and financial records.** Vehicle name and characteristics, chosen photo, odometer, fueling and charging records, expenses, maintenance, shifts, earnings, tips and fees you enter serve your calculations and private history. They are kept on the device. Eligible entries are sent to the Abastevo service only after you enable their private sync. That choice does not sync the local vehicle photo or your saved trips. Do not enter card details, security codes or banking passwords in expense fields.
- **Purchases and subscriptions, when available.** Account ID, offer, order, amount, payment status, subscription, cancellation and refund data help complete purchases and meet tax and security duties. With Pix, the provider may receive your name, CPF or CNPJ and email. With card checkout, you give the payment details directly to the processor. Do not put card numbers or security codes in contributions or support messages. The payment method and provider are disclosed before purchase. Public search and free participation do not depend on buying a plan.
- **Maps, saved items and preferences.** These include your selected city and language, settings, downloaded maps, favorites, route plans and trips you save, record, import or export. Local routes and recordings can reveal places you visited. If you export a file or open it in another app, you control where that copy goes.
- **Reports and representing a business.** We use a report's description, evidence and handling status. A request to represent a station can include identification and authorization documents or a power of attorney. A signed PDF can contain the signer's name, CPF and certificate information. These documents are private and used to review the request. Never send PFX/P12 files, passwords or private keys.
- **Support and privacy choices.** We use what you provide to support, the account or content reference, your request, proportionate identity checks and records of consent, withdrawal and deletion. When you use WhatsApp or call, the channel may disclose your number and what you choose to share.

Public sources, including ANP, RFB/OpenCNPJ, IBGE and OpenStreetMap, can supply station names, CNPJs, addresses and coordinates. Station coordinates are not a contributor's personal location. Public data identifying a natural person, including a sole proprietor, remain subject to law. You can ask the privacy team to correct the data or assess improper exposure.

## 3. Location, camera and notifications

**Selecting a city.** You can choose it manually. If you use your position, the app requests the relevant permission and may send latitude and longitude to the OpenStreetMap Foundation's Nominatim service to find the city. Nearby searches may transmit the map area required to get results.

**Navigation, trip recording and shift GPS.** When you start one of these actions, the app can use precise location during the activity, including with the screen locked or another app in front. Android shows a notification. Stop from the app or the notification. Optional recording saves the trip to your device. Shift GPS helps calculate distance. The permission does not allow publishing those movements or hidden tracking outside the activity you started.

**Camera and files.** These apply when you choose to photograph or select a receipt, vehicle photo, document or map file. We do not request access to the microphone, contacts, SMS or calls for these features. Voice navigation uses compatible local system speech synthesis without recording your voice.

**Notifications.** They cover alerts you choose and activities in progress, such as navigation. You can change permissions in device settings. Features that need a refused permission may stop working. Public searches and manual city selection remain available.

## 4. Photos, OCR and model training

Abastevo may process a photo submitted as evidence to extract and verify prices and let authorized people review it. Avoid photographing people, vehicle plates, documents or other unnecessary data. Removing EXIF metadata, including embedded GPS, does not remove information visible in the image itself.

**Temporary evidence.** The operational window for accessing a photo is limited to **24 hours** from when the server first receives it. Resending or reviewing it does not restart the window. The service blocks access at expiry and deletes temporary files through its retention routines. Access expiry and physical deletion are separate. A request acknowledgment does not certify that every copy has been deleted. You can ask whether deletion has finished.

Temporary capture copies on the device have their own validity. A powered-off device cannot clean up files. The check and removal take place when the app runs again. This does not extend access on the server. Temporary evidence rules do not determine retention for vehicle photos, saved trips or authorization documents. See [retention criteria](#8-how-long-we-keep-data).

**Google ML Kit.** Android text recognition runs on your device. According to Google's documentation, the SDK does not send the image or recognized text to Google for this processing. It may still connect to Google to download or update models and send technical data, such as device, system and app characteristics, installation or device identifiers, usage events, errors, configuration and performance. This differs from the photo you choose to attach to an Abastevo contribution. See the [ML Kit terms and privacy information](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=pt-BR).

**Abastevo model-training collection remains disabled.** Recording an optional preference and the terms version does not enable collection or select your photos. A new purpose requires an explanation of the data, processors and retention periods, plus fresh specific consent where required. We do not send contributions to a third-party AI for a new purpose based on a general authorization in this policy.

## 5. Legal bases for processing

- **Provision of the requested service and related steps.** Account, contributions, support and private features you choose to use.
- **Consent when required.** Optional choices that need a specific indication, in addition to system permissions. Opening this page, silence and pre-ticked boxes do not establish consent.
- **Legal or regulatory obligations and regular exercise of rights.** Responding to competent authorities, keeping required records and defending rights in proceedings or disputes.
- **Legitimate interest when appropriate.** Security, fraud prevention and strictly necessary operation or technical improvement, after assessing purpose, necessity and the balance with your rights. It is not a blanket authorization to collect data.

The applicable basis depends on the operation. You can ask about the purpose and legal basis of a specific use. Granting camera or location permission does not authorize advertising, model training or sharing for another purpose.

## 6. Who may receive information

- **Infrastructure and security providers.** Hosting, database, storage and recovery services, with access limited to the service purpose and protection consistent with law and this policy. Authorized staff can review data needed for support, verification and moderation.
- **Google.** Android and Play Services components and ML Kit can receive the technical data described above. When used, Google sign-in follows its own authentication purpose.
- **OpenStreetMap Foundation and Nominatim.** Coordinates or addresses queried and connection data required for geocoding. See the [OpenStreetMap Foundation policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
- **Payment processors.** Depending on the method offered, Stripe for card checkout and Asaas for Pix can receive the data needed for purchase and fraud prevention, when available. See the [Stripe policy](https://stripe.com/br/privacy) and [Asaas policy](https://central.ajuda.asaas.com/hc/pt-br/articles/32098003163035-Pol%C3%ADtica-de-Privacidade). This description does not enable charges or say that every method is available in your version.
- **WhatsApp/Meta and other destinations you choose.** Opening support channels or an external link lets the provider receive the data needed for its service. Opening this page does not start a conversation or send a request for you. See the [WhatsApp policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- **The community, authorities or a successor to the service.** Sharing is limited to content meant for publication, legal obligations or a legitimate transfer that protects rights and provides appropriate notice, as applicable.

We do not sell personal data. Private financial records, evidence photos, representation documents, precise contributor coordinates and reporters' identities are not published in portal datasets. Necessary sharing does not give a provider unrestricted permission to reuse the data.

The site is static and uses no advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts. Public search uses static pages and files instead of a search API. Connection data are still processed as described here. App SDKs may still collect the information described above.

International transfers under our responsibility require a basis and safeguards permitted by the LGPD and ANPD regulations, such as applicable contractual clauses or another valid mechanism. ## 8. How long we keep data

We keep each category as long as needed for its purpose, your choices and applicable duties. There is no universal 24-hour retention period for Abastevo data.

- **Account, preferences and private records.** Kept while needed for the account or selected feature, or until deletion, subject to legal exceptions. Device-only data are governed by local controls. Deleting a server account does not delete an exported file at another destination.
- **Temporary evidence photos and access exports.** Photos have the operational access window of up to 24 hours above. Files generated to exercise the right of access can be downloaded for up to 24 hours after they become available. Neither period guarantees remote deletion on a powered-off device.
- **Representative authorizations and powers of attorney.** These have a separate purpose and retention from price photos. The standard period for authorization documents is up to two years. Scans classified as temporary evidence have a 24-hour limit. Retention for legal duties or to defend rights is limited to what is needed and must be explained in support.
- **Purchases and payments.** Performance, reconciliation, cancellation, refunds, tax duties and defense of rights follow the legal periods for each record. Account deletion does not end a duty to retain. We state the reason and period or criterion. Processors may have separate duties described in their policy at purchase.
- **Public history and moderation.** Prices, sources and statistics may remain to preserve history without an improper personal link. Deletion requests cover account links and personal data in content. Truly anonymous information and facts from public sources are treated separately. Moderation and defense records remain restricted for as long as needed for the case, then are deleted or anonymized.
- **Security and legal duties.** Credentials and technical records have their own expiry and disposal cycles. Article 15 of Brazil's Marco Civil da Internet calls for confidential retention of app access logs — IP, date and time — for **six months** where applicable, subject to legitimate preservation orders. It does not require storing photos, conversations or trips for six months.
- **Backups and device copies.** Restricted backups support recovery and follow the environment's retention cycle and deletion requests. Deleted data should not return to use in a restoration. Loss of access in an account does not mean every backup is destroyed immediately. The operating system can include eligible local files in backup or transfer depending on settings. You may ask about timing and the status of copies of your data.

If we keep information on a legitimate basis after a deletion request, we explain its category, reason and end date or criterion. We do not reuse it for advertising or purposes incompatible with that retention.

## 9. How to exercise your rights

You may ask for confirmation and access, correction, information about data sharing, anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary, excessive or irregularly processed data, portability when applicable, deletion of data processed on consent subject to legal exceptions, and withdrawal of consent. You can object to irregular processing on another basis. You can also seek review of solely automated decisions affecting your interests and information about the criteria used, within legal limits.

Contact the [official privacy WhatsApp](https://wa.me/556696314240?text=Hello%2C%20I%20would%20like%20to%20exercise%20a%20privacy%20right%20with%20Abastevo.) or use the phone or address of the [controller](#1-who-is-responsible-for-your-data). Say the request concerns Abastevo and name the right you wish to exercise. Controller assistance is free. Identity checks are proportionate and should not require you to make data public or give up an access key.

Under the LGPD and applicable rules, confirmation or access can be provided immediately in simplified form or by a full statement within 15 days. This is not a universal deadline for physical deletion. For other requests we explain the steps, expected completion and any reason to retain the data or legal reason we cannot comply.

If the issue remains unresolved, you can petition or file a complaint with [ANPD](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/titular-de-dados/direito-dos-titulares) or use other legal avenues.

## 10. Deleting your account and associated data

In the app, use **Profile → Manage account → Delete my account** and confirm. You do not need to talk to support. To request deletion without reinstalling, use the private channel on [Delete your account and data](/en/delete-account/).

The request covers the account and associated personal data, not just temporary deactivation. After valid receipt by the server, access is revoked and unlinking and deletion start. Confirmation of the request is separate from confirmation that all procedures have finished. Offline requests may need to be sent or confirmed later.

Effectively anonymous history, legally retained data and public-source facts may remain under the [retention rules](#8-how-long-we-keep-data). Uninstalling the app does not request deletion from the server. Account deletion does not automatically cancel a subscription. Manage billing with the purchase provider. A debt does not authorize retaining unnecessary data.

## 11. Security and incident notifications

We use HTTPS, authentication for protected actions, restricted access, public and private data separation, and expiry and recovery controls. Risks remain and local files may have different encryption protections. Protect your device access and account credentials.

If an incident could create significant risk or harm to data subjects, the controller will assess and notify ANPD and affected people under the LGPD and CD/ANPD Resolution 15/2024. The regulatory limit is three business days, subject to applicable exceptions. The privacy channel also accepts reports of improper data exposure.

## 12. Children and adolescents

The service provides fuel information and features for drivers and vehicle owners. It is not presented as a children's service. Minors' protections still apply. Processing must respect their best interests, the LGPD and applicable duties under Brazil's Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents, including where access by minors is likely.

This policy is not blanket permission to collect children's data or direct behavioral advertising at minors. Guardians can contact the privacy channel for information, to report improper collection or to request appropriate action.

## 13. Updates and new features

This policy describes processing for features actually offered. Expansion to another platform or region, a new provider, billing, a new form of analysis or use of images for AI requires review before activation. These possibilities are not all currently available.

We will publish the new version with its date and flag material changes in the service through appropriate means. For new purposes we will update the information and seek specific consent or another indication when required. Continuing to use the app does not replace explicit authorization required by law or the app store.
