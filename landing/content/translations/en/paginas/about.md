## What abastevo is

abastevo is a project that makes fuel prices in Brazil visible with **source,
date and condition always in view**. It brings together three fronts:

- **Public data in the browser:** pages with
  [reference prices by state and city](/en/fuel-prices/),
  [station lookup](/en/search/) and
  [enforcement](/en/enforcement/), generated from official data
  published by ANP, Brazil's oil and fuels regulator.
- **Guides and tools:** [fuel guides](/en/guides/) and
  [free calculators](/en/tools/) that explain how the price is formed, how to
  choose a fuel and how to read the prices at the station.
- **Android app in development:** an app to check the ANP reference and, in a
  separate block, observations sent by the community. The app is not yet
  published on Google Play; see [how to use the app](/en/how-to/).

## Independence

abastevo **is not affiliated with ANP, Petrobras (Brazil's state-controlled
oil company), any government, distributor or station chain**. We use public
data that ANP makes available to any citizen, and we cite the origin of every
number.

No station, brand or advertiser pays to appear, move up in position or earn a
trust badge. Accounts and community participation are free, and any paid
features in the future will never change the trust assigned to a price.

## Where the data comes from

- **ANP Fuel Price Survey (Levantamento de Preços de Combustíveis):** a weekly
  sample survey carried out in part of the country's stations and
  municipalities. It is an official reference for the surveyed week, not the
  station's price right now. Learn the details in the guide
  [how the ANP weekly survey works](/en/guides/anp-weekly-price-survey/) and
  in the [methodology](/en/methodology/).
- **Enforcement published by ANP:** official records of infraction notices and
  fines released by the agency, presented with the source and the limits of
  what they prove.
- **Community (in the app):** observations sent by drivers, with date, time
  and payment condition. They always stay separate from the official reference
  and are never added to it.

The official data can be checked directly on the
[ANP](https://www.gov.br/anp/) website.

## abastevo Team

The guides, tools and pages of this site are written and reviewed by the
**abastevo Team**, which signs the texts collectively. The English and Spanish
versions are translations produced and reviewed with artificial intelligence.
The rules we follow to cite sources, show dates, translate and correct errors
are in the [editorial policy](/en/editorial-policy/).

## Public code

The project's source code is published in the
[abastevo repository on GitHub](https://github.com/AlexandreZanata/abastevo),
under the Abastevo Visible Attribution License: anyone can study, audit, reuse
and propose improvements, keeping visible credit to the original project.
People who help on an ongoing basis appear on the page of
[contributors](/en/contributors/).

## Privacy

Checking prices requires neither an account nor your location, and this site
does not use cookies or third-party trackers. The details are in the
[privacy policy](/en/privacy/).
