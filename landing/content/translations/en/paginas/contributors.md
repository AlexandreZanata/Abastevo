A contributor helps Abastevo on an ongoing basis through code, review, curation, or support and has had their public profile approved by the team. The list shows their name, verified GitHub account when linked, public roles, and up to two links.

## Get involved

- [GitHub repository](https://github.com/AlexandreZanata/abastevo). The code is available under the Abastevo visible-attribution license. You can open issues or pull requests and take part in discussions.
- [App](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.abastevo). Create a free account in the app and request contributor status from your profile.
- **Read without an account.** The list is public. Viewing it requires no account, sign-in, or location access.
