## What this policy is for

Fuel weighs on the budget of anyone who drives, and wrong information costs
money. This page describes the rules that the [abastevo Team](/en/about/)
follows in every guide, tool and data page of this site.

## Sources and citations

- **Primary source first.** Whenever possible, we cite the body that produces
  or regulates the information: [ANP](https://www.gov.br/anp/), Brazil's oil
  and fuels regulator; [Inmetro](https://www.gov.br/inmetro/), the national
  metrology institute; [Receita Federal](https://www.gov.br/receitafederal/),
  the federal tax authority; [CONFAZ](https://www.confaz.fazenda.gov.br/), the
  National Council for Fiscal Policy; the legislation published on
  [Planalto](https://www.planalto.gov.br/), the website of the Presidency; and
  the price composition pages of
  [Petrobras](https://precos.petrobras.com.br/), the state-controlled oil
  company.
- **A link to check.** Every important factual statement carries a link to the
  source, so you can verify it without relying on our word.
- **No invented numbers.** We do not publish statistics, prices, dates or
  percentages that are not in a cited source. When a value changes often, such
  as tax rates and blend percentages, we explain the concept and point to
  where the current value can be checked.
- **Examples marked as examples.** Calculations with fictitious prices are
  identified as illustrative.

## How we show prices and dates

- The **ANP reference** appears with its collection week. It is an official
  sample from that week, not the station's price today.
- **Community observations**, in the app, show date, time and payment
  condition, and stay in a block separate from the official reference. The two
  sources are never mixed or added together.
- We do not use "today" for weekly data, and we do not promise to point out
  the cheapest station in the region: no available source covers every station
  in real time.
- When there is no recent data for a city or fuel, the page says so instead of
  filling the space with an estimate.

The technical details are in the [methodology](/metodologia/) (in Portuguese)
and in the guide
[how the ANP weekly survey works](/en/guides/anp-weekly-price-survey/).

## Publication and review dates

Every guide shows its publication date and the date of its last update. We
review the texts when the legislation, a fuel specification or the data source
changes. A layout-only change does not alter the update date.

## Corrections

If you find an error, open an
[issue on the project's GitHub](https://github.com/AlexandreZanata/abastevo/issues)
stating the page, the passage and, if possible, the correct source. Once we
confirm the error, we correct the text and update the review date. Corrections
that change the meaning of a piece of information are recorded in a note at
the end of the page.

## Use of artificial intelligence

Artificial intelligence tools may help with research, organization and the
writing of drafts. No original text in Portuguese is published without human
review: every statement is checked against the cited sources before
publication. We do not publish mass-generated articles or pages created only
to occupy search results.

## Translations

The pages in English and Spanish are translations of the Portuguese texts.
They are produced and reviewed with artificial intelligence, without prior
human review: the review checks every number, formula and source against the
original and uses a glossary of terms. We translate only part of the site, and
every translated page points to the original.

If the versions differ, the Portuguese text prevails. When the original
changes, the translation shows a notice with the date of the change and leaves
search results until it is reviewed again. Translation errors are corrected
through the same channel as other corrections.

## Independence and paid content

- abastevo is not affiliated with ANP, Petrobras, governments, distributors or
  station chains.
- We do not publish sponsored content or paid links in the guides.
- Payment never buys position, prominence or trust for a price. Accounts and
  community participation are free.
- Brands and company names are cited only to identify products, services or
  sources.

## Authorship

The texts are signed collectively by the **abastevo Team**. More about the
project, the data sources and the public code is on the page
[about abastevo](/en/about/).
