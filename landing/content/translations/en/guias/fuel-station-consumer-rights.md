::: resumo
You have the right to see the price and payment terms before fueling and to pay the lowest price shown if the station's displays disagree for the same fuel and payment terms. You can also ask for a tax receipt, a pump check with the 20-liter measure and basic quality tests. Procon or [consumidor.gov.br](https://www.consumidor.gov.br/) handles consumer complaints. [ANP](https://www.gov.br/anp/) handles fuel quality and [Inmetro](https://www.gov.br/inmetro/) handles measurement.
:::

## A clear price before refueling

The [Brazilian Consumer Protection Code](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l8078compilado.htm) protects the right to clear information about products and prices. [Law No. 10,962/2004](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2004-2006/2004/lei/l10.962.htm) and its regulation govern how prices are displayed. At the station, look for a legible price and visible terms for each fuel before agreeing to the purchase. See [how to read the pump and sign](/en/guides/how-to-read-fuel-prices/).

## The charge must match the advertised price

The advertised offer binds the seller. If the seller's price displays disagree for the same product, Law No. 10,962/2004 provides for the lower price. Compare the sign and the pump for your chosen payment method, check that the pump starts at zero and report any mismatch before paying. A photo helps record the discrepancy.

## Different prices for card, Pix or cash

[Law No. 13,455/2017](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2017/lei/l13455.htm) allows different prices based on payment timing or method. The conditions must be clear and visible in advance. Question an undisclosed card surcharge. Loyalty-app discounts also need clear terms.

## Ask for a tax receipt with estimated taxes

Ask for the receipt as proof of purchase. It records the date, time, station, business registration number (CNPJ), product, quantity and charge. [Law No. 12,741/2012](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2011-2014/2012/lei/l12741.htm) requires approximate taxes on the document. See [how gasoline prices are formed](/en/guides/how-gasoline-prices-are-formed/) for the components.

## Volume: the 20-liter standard measure

You can ask for a pump check with the **20-liter standard measure** verified by the [Inmetro network](https://www.gov.br/inmetro/). The attendant fills the measure and compares its volume with the display. Report a discrepancy beyond the permitted tolerance to Inmetro or your state's Ipem. Use the [fill-up calculator](/en/tools/fill-up-cost-calculator/) to estimate the cost. Question a volume far beyond your vehicle's tank capacity.

## Quality checks at the station

If you suspect a fuel quality issue, ask for the applicable basic test. Examples include the graduated cylinder test for ethanol in gasoline and reading the density and temperature gauge on a hydrous ethanol pump. See [how to identify and report adulterated fuel](/en/guides/recognize-and-report-adulterated-fuel/).

## Tied sales and extra services

The Consumer Protection Code prohibits making one sale conditional on another. Additives, an oil change or cleaning can be offered separately. You choose whether to accept, and an additional service can be charged only when you agreed to it in advance.

## Refueling checklist

1. Read the sign and check which payment method its price applies to.
2. Check the grade on the pump, such as regular or additive gasoline, ethanol, Diesel S10 or S500.
3. Check that the pump starts at zero.
4. Watch the fill-up and compare the final liters and amount.
5. Compare the amount charged with the display and the advertised payment terms.
6. Ask for the receipt and verify the product and quantity.
7. Check that the fuel cap is closed before leaving.

If something differs, raise it calmly at the station, take photos and use the appropriate channel if it remains unresolved.

## Where to complain

| Issue | Contact |
| --- | --- |
| Advertised price mismatch, improper charge or tied sale | State or local Procon, or [consumidor.gov.br](https://www.consumidor.gov.br/) |
| Suspected adulterated or off-specification fuel | [ANP](https://www.gov.br/anp/) consumer channels |
| Pump volume below the display | [Inmetro](https://www.gov.br/inmetro/) or state Ipem |
| Compensation for losses or repairs | Procon, [consumidor.gov.br](https://www.consumidor.gov.br/) or small claims court |
: Complaint channels for each type of station issue

Keep the receipt, pictures of the pump and sign, and a written account with the date and time.
